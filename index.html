How to opt in

This text line, (877) 265-2010 ("4N Assistant"), sends internal operational messages only to the family partners of fourNventures, Ltd. No tenants, customers or members of the public receive these messages, and phone numbers are never purchased or imported.

A partner opts in in one of two ways:

  1. By text: text START to (877) 265-2010 from their own mobile phone.
  2. In writing: by signing the written consent form below (or emailing the consent statement) to tim.n@fournventures.com.

Consent statement: "I agree to receive operational text messages (updates, reminders and alerts) from fourNventures, Ltd. at the mobile number I provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase."

Confirmation message sent after opt-in: "4N Assistant: You're subscribed to partner updates from fourNventures, Ltd. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Written consent form:

Opt-in records (date, method, number) are kept by the partnership. See our privacy terms below.
